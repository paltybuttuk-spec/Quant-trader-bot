Quant Models
Six institutional-grade models
running simultaneously
01
Time-Series Momentum Factor
Measures returns across 5, 10, and 20-bar lookback windows simultaneously. Weights recent performance higher (0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2). The single most consistently profitable quant signal documented across 100 years of market data.
02
Z-Score Mean Reversion
Measures how many standard deviations price has moved from its 20-period mean. When Z-score exceeds ±2.0 the model signals statistical reversion probability. Acts as lead signal in ranging regimes.
03
Trend Efficiency Regime Filter
Calculates the ratio of net price displacement to total path length over 10 bars. A value above 0.55 signals trending, below 0.35 signals ranging. Determines which model leads — the most critical decision in systematic trading.
04
VWAP Institutional Filter
Calculates volume-weighted average price and measures where current price sits relative to it. Institutional traders anchor to VWAP — price above means institutions are net buyers. Acts as final confirmation before any trade setup.
05
Volatility Regime Classifier
Compares 10-day realized volatility to the 30-day average. LOW regime boosts signal confidence by 20%. HIGH regime reduces it by 30%. EXTREME regime suppresses all trade setups.
06
Sharpe-Weighted Aggregation
Instead of fixed timeframe weights, the composite score weights each timeframe by its recent Sharpe ratio. Timeframes that have been more accurate recently get more weight automatically — self-adjusting in real time.
